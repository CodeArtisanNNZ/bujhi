# Class 8 textbook sources

Chapter, unit and reading titles were transcribed from the contents pages of these NCTB 2026 textbooks. Literature authors and source numbering are preserved. Parts restart where the books restart; unnumbered composition and appendix entries remain unnumbered. Printed page ranges are used, rather than PDF viewer page numbers.

Bujhi study notes, questions and activities are original concise reading support. Complete source passages, illustrations and textbook exercises remain available through the textbook links. The existing ICT reader and its 27 named lesson sections remain intact; Science and BGS readers are retained as interactive extensions.
