# Class 8 ICT curriculum source

The names, continuous lesson numbers and printed-page ranges in `class8Ict.ts`
were checked against Bujhi's existing NCTB 2026 textbook PDF:

- [Textbook PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KJUNAQkUGMid28Oq0gJMg1WrTeMEynkm/view?usp=drivesdk)
- [NCTB 2026 Class 8 catalogue](https://nctb.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/695b9858c4774958d7b703d8)
- Edition page: revised October 2025. Checked 21 October 2026.
- PDF pages are printed page numbers plus 5. The scanned headings were visually
  checked where OCR missed or ambiguously read the Bengali numbers.

The book repeats “নেটওয়ার্কের ব্যবহার” for lessons 10 and 11. These retain
separate lesson numbers and distinct explanations. The book explicitly groups
lessons 23–43 under “স্প্রেডশিট সফটওয়্যার ব্যবহারের কৌশল” and lessons 47–69
under “ইমেইল”; do not invent separate textbook titles for every number in those
ranges or restart numbering inside each chapter.

Explanations, definitions, activities, questions and examples are original Bujhi
learning notes. They summarize concepts rather than reproduce the textbook.
The reader links to the complete textbook separately; choosing a lesson does
not open a PDF. Legal penalty amounts, specific product recommendations and
obsolete interface instructions have not been copied into the learning notes.
