# Class 8 writer directory

The directory covers the 31 original authors of stories, prose, poems, drama and travel writing in the two Class 8 Bangla literature books. Translator and adapter credits appear under the relevant original author's reading, rather than as separate writer profiles. Textbook editorial teams and anonymous compilations are not presented as literary authors.

Names, reading titles, translator/adapter credits, source parts and printed page ranges come from `class8BookContents.ts`, transcribed from these supplied NCTB textbooks:

- [সাহিত্য-কণিকা](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10cMlVaGjf1SIv0wXK8Nw_6NHArQY_iDJ/view?usp=drivesdk): 24 readings; 22 distinct individual authors, plus the compiled reading “গণঅভ্যুত্থানের কথা”. Nazrul's prose and poem share one profile.
- [আনন্দপাঠ](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1P4I27E3qVXr4AEqoRzbY0OH6gAI0kHDi/view?usp=drivesdk): nine readings, nine original authors, two translators (Giti Sen and Manabendra Bandyopadhyay), and one adapter (Akhtaruzzaman Elias).

There are 31 original authors displayed, 34 distinct credited contributors retained in the data, 33 readings including the compilation, and 35 person-to-reading credits. English name variants support search; the displayed Bangla names preserve the contents transcription.

The short introductions and reading prompts are original Bujhi learning support. They do not reproduce textbook biographies or passages. No unsourced dates, portraits or direct quotations are added. The existing book and chapter records remain the source of truth for reading links.

Additional institutional references checked for brief occupational descriptions:

- [Banglapedia: Motaher Hossain Chowdhury](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Chowdhury%2C_Motaher_Hossain)
- [Bangla Academy publication catalogue](https://esales.banglaacademy.gov.bd/books/all): Shamsuzzaman Khan's folklore publications and Bipradash Barua's publications.
