Complex Polygenic Traits & Environmental Influence

Traits like human height or skin tone do not follow simple Mendelian 1-locus rules. They are influenced by multiple gene loci (Genes A, B, C, D) acting additively, along with continuous environmental variables (nutrition, sunlight).

Core Concept Reminders

  • Homologous Pair: Offspring receive exactly 1 chromosome of each pair from P1 and 1 from P2.
  • Allele vs Gene: A gene is the sequence location (locus); alleles ($A, a$) are variations of that sequence.
  • Punnett Square: Predicts probabilities across many offspring, not guaranteed individual birth outcomes!
