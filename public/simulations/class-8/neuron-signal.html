Sensory vs Motor Neuron Comparison

Sensory Neuron

  • Direction: Receptor → Central Nervous System (CNS)
  • Function: Carries sensory nerve impulses detected by receptors toward the spinal cord.
  • Cell Body Location: Dorsal Root Ganglion (outside CNS).
  • Dendrites: Long dendron connecting to receptor endings.

Motor Neuron

  • Direction: CNS → Effector (Muscle / Gland)
  • Function: Carries motor commands away from the CNS to activate responses.
  • Cell Body Location: Grey matter of the spinal cord (ventral horn).
  • Axon: Long axon terminating at neuromuscular junctions.
Key Takeaway: Information flows strictly in one direction through each specialized neuron type, maintaining the structural pathway: Receptor → Sensory Neuron → CNS → Motor Neuron → Effector.

Synaptic Transmission Detail

1. Electrical Wave Arrives: The electrical action potential reaches the end of the presynaptic axon terminal.

2. Neurotransmitter Release: Chemical messengers (neurotransmitters) are released from vesicles into the synaptic cleft.

3. Chemical Diffusion: Chemicals diffuse across the tiny gap and bind to receptor molecules on the next neuron, initiating a new electrical impulse.
